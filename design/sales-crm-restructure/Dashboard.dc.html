The last row is No for everyone. Financial records use Void, Cancelled or Reversed instead of deletion, always.
The real roles in the system are manager · super_admin · admin · closer · sales · hr · marketing · staff · read_only · agency_user. The seven above must be mapped onto that set — no new values in the enum — and permissions are checked with user.role, not activeRole.
