FIVE STATUSES THAT MUST STAY SEPARATE — never one field with twenty values

WHEN A SOLD DEAL IS CREATED

Reservation→
Confirm Sale→
Sold Deal
Trigger: an authorised approver confirms the sale and the mandatory data from the wizard is complete. It does not mean everything is paid. It means a commercial sale has been confirmed and a formal record now exists.
Never use the deposit as the only trigger. A customer who has signed but not yet paid would have nowhere to hold the contract, the schedule or the commission — a circular dependency.
